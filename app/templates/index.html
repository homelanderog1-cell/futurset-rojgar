Aspirant Fee Advisory (Post Office vs Online Cyber-Receipt)

When paying OJAS fees via Post Office, ensure the computerized barcode challan is stamped before 3:00 PM on working days. For online payments (Debit Card / NetBanking / UPI), wait for the final "Success" redirect and download the e-Receipt immediately. Do not attempt multiple payments within 15 minutes of a pending status.

OJAS Gujarat Online Application Guide

Official step-by-step instructions for ojas.gujarat.gov.in

1

One-Time Registration (OTR)

Visit ojas.gujarat.gov.in and click on "Registration" → "Apply". Enter your full name as per SSC Marksheet, Date of Birth, Gender, Category (SEBC/SC/ST/EWS), Mobile Number, and Aadhaar card details.

2

Upload Photo & Signature (Strict Specifications)

Passport Photograph: File size must be under 15 KB, JPG format, Dimensions: 5 cm height x 3.6 cm width.
Signature: File size must be under 15 KB, JPG format, Dimensions: 2.5 cm height x 7.5 cm width.

3

Apply for Advertisement & Confirm

Go to "Online Application" → "Apply". Select the Department (e.g. GPRB for Police, GSSSB, GPSC). Fill in educational qualifications. Click "Confirm Application" to generate your unique 8-digit Confirmation Number.

4

Fee Payment & Print Hall Ticket

For General category candidates, pay ₹100 + bank charges online via NetBanking/UPI or Post Office Challan. SC/ST/SEBC/EWS/Ex-SM are exempted from examination fee in most Gujarat boards.
